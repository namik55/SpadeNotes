Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

01
Instant capture

Open, write, close. No syncing dialogs, no upsells. SpadeNotes opens in milliseconds and gets out of your way.

02
Rich text editor

Headings, bold, italic, links, checklists, code blocks, images, and citations — everything a student or researcher needs.

03
Local-first

Your notes live as plain files on your machine. No cloud. No account. No subscription. Always yours.

04
Calendar built-in

See your dated notes on a calendar. Add quick notes to any date. No separate planner needed.

05
Notebooks & folders

Organize notes into nested notebooks. Drag and drop to rearrange. Color-coded for quick scanning.

06
A4 page canvas

Write on a real A4 page with proper margins. Export to PDF exactly as it looks. Perfect for seminar papers.

Free & Open Source

SpadeNotes is completely free and open source. Built by a law student who wanted a calmer way to take notes. The code is on GitHub — read it, fork it, contribute to it.
